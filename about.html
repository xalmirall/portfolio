Curious about people since before I knew it was a job.

I'm Xavi, a User Experience Designer with a Psychology background. Since I was a child, I have been interested in technology. Still, my curiosity in human behavior led me to study Psychology and after six years as a Tech Recruiter I decided to take a step forward in my career and graduate in UX/UI Design.

I believe in empowering individuals with technology by designing digital products with data-driven solutions. I guide myself by responsibility, honesty, commitment, perseverance, and freedom values.

On the other hand, whenever I can, I try to escape from the monotony and take off with my other passion: music, bestowed by innumerable people and places. Fear doesn't afflict me, and I enjoy involving myself in new challenging projects to further my learning 🚀
